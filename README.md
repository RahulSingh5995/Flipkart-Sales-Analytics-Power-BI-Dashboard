# Flipkart-Sales-Analytics-Power-BI-Dashboard
This project focuses on analyzing Flipkart sales data using Microsoft Power BI to understand sales performance, customer distribution, profitability, payment preferences, and geographic patterns.

The objective was to transform the available sales data into an interactive business dashboard that allows users to explore performance across different dates, cities, categories, states, and sub-categories.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of key sales and customer-related metrics and helps identify high-performing categories, profitable sub-categories, major customer regions, and monthly sales and profit trends.

🎯 Project Objectives
   1. Analyze overall Flipkart sales performance.
   2. Understand sales distribution across different product categories.
   3. Analyze monthly sales and profit trends.
   4. Identify the most profitable product sub-categories.
   5. Understand customer distribution across different categories.
   6. Analyze customer distribution by state.

📊 Dashboard Components

The dashboard contains the following major analytical sections.

1. Date Filter

A date-range filter was added to allow users to analyze sales performance for a selected period.

The dashboard covers the period:

01-Jan-2018 to 25-Dec-2018

Users can adjust the date range to analyze the dashboard based on the selected period.

2. City Filter

A city slicer was added to allow users to filter the complete dashboard based on a selected city.

This makes it possible to analyze:

Category performance
Customer distribution
Sales and profit trends
Geographic distribution
Profitability

for different locations.

3. Amount by Category

A category-wise column chart was created to compare the total sales amount across major product categories.

The dashboard currently analyzes:

Electronics
Clothing
Furniture

This visualization helps identify which product categories contribute the most to overall sales.

4. Monthly Distribution – Profit vs Sales

A monthly trend visualization was created to compare sales amount and profit throughout the year.

The chart allows users to observe:

Monthly sales movement
Monthly profit movement
High and low-performing months
Changes in profitability over time
Differences between sales volume and profit

This helps understand how business performance changes throughout the year.

5. Geo Distribution

A geographic map was included to visualize the distribution of customers/orders across different locations in India.

This provides a geographical perspective of the business and helps identify regions where customer activity is concentrated.

6. Orders by Payment Mode

A donut chart was created to analyze the distribution of orders across different payment methods.

The dashboard includes:

UPI
COD
EMI
Credit Card
Debit Card

This helps understand customer payment preferences and the contribution of different payment methods to total orders.

7. Customers by Category

A category-wise customer distribution visualization was created to understand the proportion of customers associated with different product categories.

The dashboard compares:

Electronics
Clothing
Furniture

This provides an overview of customer distribution across the major product categories.

8. Top 10 States by Customers

A treemap was created to identify the top 10 states based on customer count.

This visualization makes it easier to compare the relative customer presence across different states.

It helps identify important geographic markets and regions with higher customer concentration.

9. Top 5 Profitable Sub-Categories

A horizontal bar chart was created to identify the top five most profitable product sub-categories.

The current dashboard highlights:

Printers
Bookcases
Saree
Accessories
Tables

This analysis helps identify the product segments contributing strongly to profitability.

🎛️ Interactive Features

The dashboard includes interactive elements that allow users to explore the data dynamically.

Date Selection

Users can select a specific date range to update the dashboard.

City Selection

Users can select a city from the city slicer to filter the dashboard.

Cross-Filtering

Power BI visuals interact with each other, allowing users to explore different aspects of the dataset through selections and filters.
